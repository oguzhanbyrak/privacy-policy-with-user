Comic Runner – Privacy Policy

Developer: Oguzhan Bayrak · Contact: oguzhan.byrk@gmail.com · Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Comic Runner (iOS and Android, the "App") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The App does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Game account – an anonymous account identifier created automatically on first launchTo save your progress and link it to your purchases
Player name you choose (shown on public leaderboards)Leaderboards. Offensive names are rejected automatically.
Game data – scores, distance, gold and gem balances, owned skins, upgrades, daily rewards, missions and weekly rankTo run the game and the leaderboards
Purchase records – which gem pack was bought and the store transaction ID (no payment card details – payments are handled by Apple / Google)To deliver purchased gems exactly once
Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android) and basic device informationShowing rewarded video ads and measuring them (Google AdMob)
Settings stored on your device (sound, music, vibration, reminders)Your preferences. These stay on your device.

2. Third-party services

  • Supabase – stores your game account and game data (privacy policy).
  • Google AdMob – optional rewarded video ads (privacy policy, how Google uses data from ads).
  • RevenueCat – processes in-app purchase receipts so purchased gems can be delivered (privacy policy).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – handle all payments.
  • Expo – the framework the App is built with.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Ads, tracking and consent

Ads are only shown when you choose to watch a video (to continue a run or spin the bonus wheel). On iOS the App asks for permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before ads are loaded. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads).

4. In-app purchases

Gems can be bought with real money through the App Store or Google Play. We receive a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction ID) – never your payment details.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules reminders on your device (daily reward, weekly leaderboard). They are created locally; you can turn them off in the App's settings or in your device settings.

6. Deleting your data

In the App: open Settings › Delete Account and confirm. Your account and all of its data (name, scores, balances, skins, upgrades, purchase records, leaderboard entries) are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

By email: if you no longer have the App, email oguzhan.byrk@gmail.com with your player name and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Deleting the account cannot be undone, and purchased items cannot be restored afterwards.

7. Data retention

We keep your game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our database. Advertising and purchase partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the email above to use these rights.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email oguzhan.byrk@gmail.com – please include your player name and device model. We usually reply within 2 business days.
