3. Ads, tracking and consent

Ads are only shown when you choose to watch a video in exchange for a reward. On iOS the Game asks for permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before ads are loaded. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads).

4. In-app purchases

Items can be bought with real money through the App Store or Google Play. We receive a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction ID), never your payment details.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, a Game may schedule reminders on your device (for example a daily reward). They are created locally; you can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

6. Deleting your data

In the Game: use the delete option listed for that Game in Section 11 and confirm. Your account and all of its data (name, scores, balances, items, upgrades, purchase records, leaderboard entries) are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

By email: if you no longer have the Game, email oguzhan.byrk@gmail.com with the Game's name and your player name, and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Deleting an account cannot be undone, and purchased items cannot be restored afterwards.

7. Data retention

We keep your game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our database. Advertising and purchase partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

8. Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the email above to use these rights.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
